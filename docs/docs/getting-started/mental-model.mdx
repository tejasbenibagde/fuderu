# The Art Studio Mental Model 🎨

If you are new to canvas graphics or creative tools, words like _offscreen composite rasterization_, _bitmask clip paths_, and _sparse patch state delta serialization_ sound intimidating.

**Fuderu does not require you to be a graphics math wizard.**

To understand how Fuderu works, imagine you are sitting at an **artist's workbench in a real-world art studio**. Every single class and feature in Fuderu directly corresponds to a physical tool on that desk.

---

## The Physical Analogies

| Real-World Art Studio Tool                 | Fuderu Class / Feature | What It Does (In Plain English)                                                                                                                                                                 |
| :----------------------------------------- | :--------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Easel & Desk**                       | `Canvas`               | The main workstation that holds your artwork, listens for your hand/pen movements, and coordinates all the tools.                                                                               |
| **Clear Acetate Sheets**                   | `Layer`                | Stacked transparent plastic sheets. Drawing on sheet #2 doesn't ruin sheet #1 underneath. You can slide them, hide them, or adjust their see-through opacity.                                   |
| **The Artist's Pen / Stylus**              | `Brush`                | The physical pen you hold in your hand. Controls ink color, thickness, softness, pressure sensitivity, and tip shapes.                                                                          |
| **Coloring Inside the Lines (Stenciling)** | `Alpha Lock`           | A magical stencil that only lets paint stick to places where ink already exists. Transparent areas refuse to accept new paint.                                                                  |
| **Blue Painter's Masking Tape**            | `Selection`            | Taping off a section of your paper with rectangular, oval, or freehand tape. Any brush strokes or paint buckets only affect the taped-in area. Animated "marching ants" show the tape boundary. |
| **Floating Paper Cutout**                  | `TransformSession`     | Lifting a piece of artwork off the page so you can slide it around, rotate it with your fingers, shrink/stretch it by its corners, and press it back down when satisfied.                       |
| **The Decal Tray (Cut, Copy, Paste)**      | `ClipboardStore`       | Cutting out a sticker with scissors or copying a drawing onto transfer paper, then sticking it onto another layer or into an entirely separate sketchbook.                                      |
| **The Time Machine Film Reel**             | `HistoryManager`       | An automated film reel that remembers every stroke, cut, and paste. Lets you step backwards (`undo`) or forwards (`redo`) without re-drawing.                                                   |
| **The Polaroid Snapshot**                  | `CanvasSnapshot`       | Frozen photo snapshots of your easel's current state that you can safely hand over to React, Vue, or Svelte without them accidentally modifying your active canvas.                             |
| **The Time-Lapse Video**                   | `ActionReplay`         | A recorded log of every single pen movement and command that can be played back like a movie at 1x, 2x, or instant speed.                                                                       |

## 1. The Easel (`Canvas`)

When you create a `Canvas` instance, you are setting up your physical desk:

```typescript
import { Canvas } from "fuderu";

const painter = new Canvas({
  canvas: "#my-canvas",
  document: { width: 1200, height: 800 },
});
```

The easel handles three jobs automatically:

1. **Listens to your hand**: Intercepts mouse, touch, and stylus pen events with zero jitter.
2. **Maintains crisp resolution**: Handles high-DPI (Retina) screens automatically so your artwork never looks blurry.
3. **Coordinates tools**: Switches between your brush, eraser, selection marquee, shape tools, and transforms.

---

## 2. Clear Acetate Sheets (`Layers`)

Instead of painting on a single piece of wood, professional artists paint on transparent celluloid sheets (like classic animation cels).

- You can have a **"Sketch"** layer on the bottom.
- An **"Ink / Lineart"** layer in the middle.
- A **"Color"** layer beneath the ink.
- A **"Shading"** layer with _Multiply_ blend mode on top.

```typescript
// Create a new clear sheet
const colorLayer = painter.createLayer("Coloring");

// Set it as the active sheet you are painting on
painter.setActiveLayer(colorLayer.id);

// Adjust its see-through transparency (50%)
painter.updateLayer(colorLayer.id, { opacity: 0.5 });
```

---

## 3. The Magical Stencil (`Alpha Lock`)

Have you ever tried coloring a character's shirt, but accidentally drew outside the lines onto the background?

`alphaLock` acts as a magical stencil:

```typescript
painter.updateLayer(colorLayer.id, { alphaLock: true });
```

When `alphaLock` is turned **on**, new paint will **only stick to pixels that already have paint**. All empty, transparent areas on that sheet are completely protected.

---

## 4. Blue Painter's Masking Tape (`Selection`)

When painting a wall or canvas, you use blue painter's tape to protect specific areas:

```typescript
// Tape off a 200x150 rectangular window
painter.selectRectangle(50, 50, 200, 150);
```

- Any brush strokes, eraser marks, bucket fills, or text stamps will **only** affect what is inside the tape.
- The tape boundary is rendered as animated, dashed **marching ants**.
- When you are done, pull off the tape: `painter.clearSelection()`.

---

## 5. Floating Paper Cutouts (`Transform`)

Sometimes you draw an eye or a flower, and realize it is slightly too small or tilted:

```typescript
// 1. Lift the pixels off the canvas into your hands
painter.beginTransform();

// 2. Rotate it slightly (radians) and make it 20% bigger
painter.rotate(0.15);
painter.scale(1.2, 1.2);

// 3. Stick it back down onto the layer
painter.commitTransform();
```

While in transform mode, Fuderu renders interactive **bounding handles**, a **rotator grip**, and a **center pivot**. If you change your mind, `painter.cancelTransform()` puts the cutout back exactly where it came from.

---

## 6. The Decal Tray (`Clipboard`)

Just like cutting out paper stickers:

```typescript
// Cut the selected drawing into the decal tray
painter.cut();

// Switch to another layer or document
painter.setActiveLayer(backgroundLayer.id);

// Stick the decal onto the new layer as a brand new layer
painter.paste({ createLayer: true, newLayerName: "Decal" });
```

The clipboard is shared globally across layers and even between separate canvas documents on the same web page.

---

## 7. The Polaroid Snapshot (`CanvasSnapshot`)

Modern frontend frameworks like **React**, **Vue**, and **Svelte** get unhappy when internal objects change behind their back.

Fuderu solves this by giving you immutable **Polaroid snapshots**:

```typescript
const snapshot = painter.getSnapshot();

console.log(snapshot.layers); // Read-only list of all layers
console.log(snapshot.history.canUndo); // true or false
console.log(snapshot.selection); // Current selection bounds
```

Whenever you draw, change a layer, or make a selection, Fuderu emits a state change event. You can feed `painter.getSnapshot()` straight into React's `useSyncExternalStore()` without worrying about accidental state mutation.

---

## Summary

That's the entire architecture of Fuderu:

1. **Easel** (`Canvas`) holds your **Sheets** (`Layers`).
2. Your **Pen** (`Brush`) paints on the active sheet.
3. **Masking Tape** (`Selection`) limits where paint can go.
4. **Cutouts** (`Transform`) let you slide and scale things before stamping them down.
5. **Decals** (`Clipboard`) let you move artwork around freely.
6. The **Time Machine** (`HistoryManager`) lets you undo mistakes fearlessly.
